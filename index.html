Research

MRes thesis · Macquarie University · 2026

When Do Random Survival Forests Enhance Fraud Detection? A Conditions-Based Comparison

Random survival forests are often proposed for fraud detection, but rarely tested against the conditions that should make them useful. This thesis compares them with logistic regression, Cox models, random forests and count models on two contrasting datasets, a transaction-level card dataset and an account-level dataset, to work out when a time-to-event framing earns its place.

Supervised by Prof. Samuel Müller and A/Prof. Maurizio Manuguerra.
